SCIENCE AND GEOMETRY LIMITS

The default reactor template uses synthetic species A and B. Both have molecular weight
0.028 kg/mol and constant heat capacity 30 J/(mol K). These are original teaching
parameters, not measured properties of real chemicals. Allowed input ranges are
numerical guardrails, not experimentally validated physical ranges.

There are exactly three thermodynamic models: ideal gas, ideal solution using
Raoult's law, and Modified UNIFAC 2.0. Six sample chemical records are bundled.
Real-mixture phase results and the single TP separation use the selected model.
See THERMODYNAMICS.txt for parameter ranges, extrapolation, source notices,
caloric approximations and the future database provider boundary.

Reference temperature: 298.15 K. Species A formation enthalpy is defined as zero;
species B is -20000 J/mol. Stream enthalpy rate in W is
  H = (FA + FB) Cp (T - 298.15) + FB (-20000).
Ideal gas volumetric flow in m3/s is F R T / P, with
R = 8.314462618 J/(mol K). This is not a liquid volume estimate.

Feed: user-specified flow, composition, temperature and pressure.
Product: terminal stream sink.
Mixer: two physical inlets; component and enthalpy sums; minimum inlet pressure.
Heater/Cooler: outlet-temperature mode computes selected-model enthalpy duty;
specified-duty mode solves temperature for ideal gas only. Wrong-direction
targets, wrong-sign duties and unsupported calculated states are rejected.
PBR: an isothermal first-order synthetic reaction with two available bases.
New units integrate dFA/dW=-k(T)CA against catalyst mass, where
  CA = FA P/(Ftotal R T), k450 = 0.01 m3/(kg-cat s) by default.
The profile is checked against X=1-exp[-k(T)PW/(Ftotal RT)]. Multiple feeds
combine by component and enthalpy balance in an upstream mixer before entering
the reactor through its single inlet. Existing projects and
the original recycle template retain residence time in seconds:
  k(T) = 0.1 exp[-40000/R (1/T - 1/450)] s^-1
  X = 1 - exp(-k tau); extent = FA,in X
Duty includes reaction heat and sensible heating. There is no bed-geometry
calculation, pressure drop, transport resistance, changing residence time with flow,
kinetic regression, equilibrium reversal or mechanical sizing.
Splitter: equal composition in both outlets. The secondary fraction is bounded
0..0.95. It recycles A and B together, not selectively unreacted A.
Legacy distillation (no explicit FUG/MESH configuration): one ideal binary equilibrium contact with fixed distillate cut d and
constant relative volatility alpha. Solve zA = d*yA + (1-d)*xA with
  yA = alpha*xA / (1 + (alpha-1)*xA).
Bisection conserves component flows. It assumes equal outlet temperature and the
same sensible enthalpy model; no latent heat, stage count, reflux, saturation
properties, azeotropes, reboiler or condenser energy prediction. A zero modeled
duty does NOT mean a real separation requires no energy.
Explicit FUG and MESH modes now support multistage shortcut design and caloric
equilibrium-stage calculations respectively. See DISTILLATION.txt. FUG has no
stage profiles or separate end duties. MESH uses low-pressure VLE (Raoult or
Modified UNIFAC), ideal trays, one feed, a total or equilibrium partial condenser
and kettle reboiler;
no tray efficiency, side draws or liquid–liquid stability search. Optional
post-solve sieve-tray hydraulics reports flooding/weeping from tower geometry
and supplied liquid density/surface tension. It does not feed back into the
separation, pressure profile or duties; see DISTILLATION.txt and AI_GUIDE.txt.

Recycle: automatically tear a stream returning to a mixer, topologically solve,
and use direct substitution for up to 500 iterations. Convergence requires the
maximum scaled change in A flow, B flow, T and P to be below 1e-9. Multiple
independent cycles are rejected. Non-convergence is displayed honestly; values
are the last iterate. Mass and energy residuals are independently reported.

3D: schematic source/sink arrows, manifolds, heat exchangers, packed vessels,
tray columns and pipes derived from graph positions and material ports.
No dimensions, collisions, pressure-vessel certification, fabrication data or
mechanical CAD claims. A WebGL fallback preserves the 2D editor.

Deferred: the planned approximately 2,000-chemical database; validated
real-fluid models; selective recovery model; multiple recycle loops;
industrial column hydraulics and efficiencies; sketch import; image/segmentation AI; public AI hosting;
arbitrary pipe rerouting; full multi-selection UI; multitab edit
conflict resolution.

Rainbow flow is an illustrative stream animation; it does not encode calculated
flow speed or composition. Reduced-motion preferences stop the moving dashes.

Storage: multiple local projects per browser origin, one open at a time. Each
project supports up to 250 revisions in a version-1 JSON envelope. The IndexedDB
version-2 library preserves existing single-project saves. Imported project files
retain their included history; restore appends snapshots without deleting later
work. Clearing the current project leaves other projects available. Export before
clearing storage. No cross-device sync, encryption, accounts, folders/trash, or
multitab conflict resolution. Unknown project-file versions are rejected.
