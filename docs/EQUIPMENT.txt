EQUIPMENT MODELS

Eight operations are available: Feed, Product, Mixer, Splitter, Heater, Cooler,
Packed-bed reactor and simplified Distillation. All calculations are steady
state. Molar flows are mol/s, temperatures K, pressures Pa and heat duties W.
The 2D symbols and 3D shapes are schematic, not mechanical dimensions.

Feed and Product
Feed sets total molar flow F, mole fractions zi, temperature and pressure:
  Fi = F zi; sum(zi) = 1.
Catalog projects support 2–40 components. Legacy binary feeds retain fraction zA.
Product is a terminal material sink and reports its incoming stream unchanged.
One connection per port prevents accidental duplication of material.

Mixing and splitting
Mixer accepts a primary inlet and an optional second physical inlet:
  Fi,out = sum(Fi,in); Hout = sum(Hin).
With ideal gas and constant component heat capacities, the outlet temperature
is solved from this enthalpy balance, including formation enthalpies. It is
not generally the arithmetic average of inlet temperatures. For liquid models,
a pressure-enthalpy flash solves over the active components' supported
temperature intervals. Mixing or phase change can put the outlet outside the
inlet-temperature bracket. Unsupported enthalpy targets and data gaps are
rejected. At saturation, the energy balance determines the vapor fraction,
which is retained through downstream splitters and phase separators.
Pressure is the minimum pressure of the flowing inlets. An empty recycle guess
does not set the pressure of a real feed. No pump, pressure recovery or shaft
work is modeled.

Splitter uses a secondary fraction f from 0 to 0.95:
  Fi,out = (1-f) Fi,in; Fi,out2 = f Fi,in.
Both outputs retain the inlet composition, temperature and pressure. Q=0.
The upper bound leaves a primary outlet in the one-loop recycle examples.
This is a proportional split, not selective recovery of unreacted A.

Heating and cooling
Choose one active specification: outlet temperature or heat duty. The inactive
field is retained for switching modes but is not used in the calculation.
  Q = Hout - Hin.
For specified duty with the ideal-gas method:
  Tout = Tin + Q / sum(Fi Cp,i).
Heater duty is nonnegative and Cooler duty is nonpositive. Opposite-direction
temperature targets, invalid calculated temperatures, and nonzero duty on an
empty stream are rejected. Zero duty on an empty stream leaves its temperature.
Specified-duty mode currently supports ideal gas only. Temperature mode uses
the selected enthalpy method, including its documented phase approximations.
There is no area sizing, heat-transfer coefficient, utility circuit or pressure
drop. Supported calculated temperatures remain inside 250-800 K.

Packed-bed reactor
New reactors use a configurable reaction set. Existing projects keep their
synthetic catalyst-mass or residence-time mode until explicitly switched.

Define reactants and products with positive coefficients in the corresponding
editor tables. Internally, negative coefficients consume species and positive
coefficients produce them. Each reaction has its own basis reactant and rate:
  dFi/dW = sum_j(nu_ij r_j / abs(nu_basis,j)).
Rates describe net disappearance of the basis species in mol/(kg-cat s).
Enabled reactions are integrated together in extent coordinates using adaptive
Dormand-Prince 5(4); disabled reactions remain saved and contribute zero extent.
Independent feeds may connect to the same reactor inlet. The inlet mixer
conserves every component and enthalpy at the lowest flowing inlet pressure.

Simple laws use r = k(T) C_basis^n with any order from 0 to 20. General laws use
species tables or bounded arithmetic expressions with concentrations, partial
pressures, mole fractions, mass concentrations or mass fractions; explicit
reverse rates and adsorption/inhibition denominators are available. Rate and
species units are selectable. Changing units or order requires constants
consistent with the new law; constants are never fitted automatically.
  k(T) = k(Tref) exp[-E/R (1/T - 1/Tref)].
The expression parser supports arithmetic, powers, exp, log/ln, sqrt, abs,
min, max and pow, plus T (K), P (Pa) and R. Expressions cannot execute code.

The reaction model is isothermal gas only, using the ideal-gas thermodynamic
model. Local volume flow is Ftotal R T / P, or an explicitly specified constant
volume flow. Constant-flow mode neglects gas expansion. A prescribed pressure
drop is linear along the bed and must leave positive outlet pressure.

Specify catalyst mass to calculate conversion, or a target disappearance of a
chosen inlet reactant to calculate required mass (up to 1e9 kg). Sizing finds
the first resolved crossing of the target and re-solves the full pressure
profile when a total pressure drop is prescribed. Targets must be strictly
between zero and complete conversion. Equilibrium limits, unavailable feed,
nonphysical depletion and unresolved stiff kinetics produce errors.

Q = Hout - Hin includes formation and sensible enthalpy. Real reacting species
require supported neutral molecular formulae and formation enthalpies. Atom
and molecular-mass balance are checked before calculation. The UI reports
species inlet/outlet flows, extents, conversion, duty, outlet pressure and
catalyst mass. The conversion profile and CSV include the integrated states.
No phase equilibrium, transport resistance, catalyst deactivation, hydraulic
bed sizing or mechanical design is implied by this model.

Legacy synthetic modes retain A -> B, equal 0.028 kg/mol molecular weights,
Cp=30 J/(mol K), formation enthalpies 0 and -20000 J/mol, and E=40000 J/mol.
Catalyst-mass mode uses k450=0.01 m3/(kg-cat s) and X=1-exp[-kPW/(Ftotal RT)].
Residence-time mode uses k450=0.1 s^-1 and X=1-exp[-k tau]. Pressure is constant
and multiple feeds must be combined in an upstream mixer for these modes.

Simplified separation
With ideal gas, Distillation represents one binary equilibrium contact:
  yA = alpha*xA / (1+(alpha-1)*xA)
  zA = d*yA + (1-d)*xA.
The fixed top fraction d and relative volatility alpha determine the two
component-balanced outlet streams through a bounded scalar solve. This contact
uses the same sensible enthalpy basis at the inlet T/P; its zero modeled duty
is not a prediction of real condenser or reboiler energy.

With either liquid model, the unit separates the equilibrium vapor and liquid
at the inlet T/P using the selected model's flash. Component and phase energy
closure are checked. Unsupported phase endpoints or ambiguous saturation are
rejected instead of inventing a phase amount or heat duty. There are no stages,
reflux, column hydraulics, reboiler/condenser design or azeotrope-breaking claims.

Examples and verification
Templates cover heating/cooling, two-feed mixing/splitting, a two-feed catalyst
reactor, the legacy reactor recycle and both simplified separation methods.
tests/equipment-physics.test.ts checks independent analytical and edge cases;
tests/equipment-flowsheets.test.ts checks connected balances and compatibility;
tests/equipment-setup.test.ts checks validated actions and ports;
tests/e2e/equipment.spec.ts checks editing, persistence, profile and server API.

not experimental validation of the synthetic kinetic constants.

components have equal relative volatility in the ideal-gas teaching contact.
Liquid models perform a multicomponent TP flash with species conservation.
